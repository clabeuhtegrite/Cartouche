The first Cartouche Installer: it puts Cartouche on an iPhone from a Mac or a Windows PC, in three steps on one window.

- Plug in the iPhone, sign in with your Apple Account, a free one being enough, and press **Install Cartouche**. The installer downloads the latest Cartouche from its release and checks it, installs it under its own App ID with Increased Memory Limit, gives it its certificate, and puts the pairing file in it. Cartouche then opens on its setup with both in place: you only sign in to its SideStore.
- Installing again updates Cartouche and keeps its games and saves. An IPA of Cartouche on the computer can be installed too, and the pairing file put back in Cartouche after the iPhone was erased.
- In English and French.

Downloads:

- **Mac**, Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later: `Cartouche-Installer-0.1.0-Mac.dmg`. The app is not notarized: the first time, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows** 10 or 11: `Cartouche-Installer-0.1.0-Windows.exe`. If SmartScreen stops it, **More info › Run anyway**. Windows needs **Apple Devices** from the Microsoft Store, or iTunes, to reach the iPhone.
