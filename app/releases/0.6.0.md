Your games and saves on an S3 bucket of yours.

- Storage: System › Storage › Add S3 storage reaches a private bucket on Amazon S3, or on a server of your own such as MinIO on your NAS, with your access key. Your games folder and saves folder can be there, as on a shared folder.
- Storage: every request is signed with your keys, and the secret key stays in this iPhone's keychain. Cartouche reaches no public bucket.
- Storage: type http:// before the address of a server on your network without HTTPS; Cartouche uses HTTPS otherwise.
