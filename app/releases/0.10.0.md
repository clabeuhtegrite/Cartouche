A game waits behind the console, your Mac can be your storage, and a guide takes you to your first game.

- Games: the Home button, on screen or on a controller, brings the console back without ending the game, which waits behind it, paused. Resume goes back to it, Quit ends it. A game that needs more memory than it gets there starts in Cartouche's place, as before.
- Games: a game starts in your iPhone's language when it has it. Its settings can choose another.
- Games: Cartouche runs in iOS's Game Mode, for the most of the CPU and GPU and the lowest latency of your controllers. iOS says so when it comes on.
- Games: a game's sheet shows your play time, your sessions and when you last played, and the room the game takes on this iPhone and in your games folder. The home shows them under the game in focus.
- Home: your games alone, on one shelf, with larger covers. Shorter texts everywhere, and dark System pages. On an unfolded iPhone Duo, the home and the sheet of the game in focus stand on either side of the fold.
- Storage: System › Storage › Add your Mac (Cartouche Builder) pairs Cartouche with Cartouche Builder on your Mac, once, with the same six digits on both screens. Your games then install from the Mac and your saves go to it, encrypted on the way.
- Storage: away from home or off your VPN, a server that does not answer shows as offline within seconds, with no error, and your saves wait until it answers again. An install that the network cuts off for a moment picks up where it stopped.
- Errors: under each error, Copy the report gives what it takes to understand it, never a password or an address. System › Report a problem shows a report in full and sends nothing until you choose how: Send to Cartouche, share it, copy it, or open it on GitHub.
- Setup: on a new install, Your first game takes you from LocalDevVPN to your first game and ticks each step by itself. It stays at the top of the home until a game is installed.
- Catalog: All games lists the games nxnative added since 0.9.0 and marks the 64 it plays on the iPhone. New games get their covers within a day, without a new version of Cartouche.
- Game measurements: after each game session, Cartouche sends what nxnative measured, such as the frame rate and the memory, with its own events, and never your files, saves, accounts or the addresses of your storage. It does so unless you turn it off in System › Game measurements, which also shows the last report and erases what this iPhone sent. All that is sent: https://github.com/clabeuhtegrite/Cartouche/blob/main/docs/privacy.md
