- Measurements: each error Cartouche sends says its cause, from a fixed list, the step it stopped at, how far it got and how long it took. For the Apple ID sign-in, the renewal and the update of Cartouche, which the built-in SideStore runs, it also says where SideStore stopped and Apple's code, and a sign-in that goes through says how long it took. - Reports: Copy the report, under an error, now gives the error's cause too.
