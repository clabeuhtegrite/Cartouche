Your games and saves on an SFTP server of yours.

- Storage: System › Storage › Add an SFTP server reaches a server of yours, such as your NAS's or your Mac's, with your account on it. Your games folder and saves folder can be there, as on a shared folder.
- Storage: Cartouche keeps the server's key when you save it and shows its fingerprint. Should the server show another key later, Cartouche stops before sending your password; if you reinstalled the server, remove it in Cartouche and add it again. Your password stays in this iPhone's keychain.
