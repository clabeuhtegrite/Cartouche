Your own storage, the games' covers, and Cartouche's screens only.

- Storage: keep your games and your saves on a shared folder of your Mac, PC or NAS (SMB), or on a folder in Files, with your own account. Set it up in System, Storage; the password stays in this iPhone's keychain.
- The games in your games folder show in All games with Install from: Cartouche unpacks the IPA as it arrives, so the iPhone never holds both. Copy to puts an installed game there as an IPA, and Uninstall offers to copy it first.
- Your saves are copied to your saves folder after each game, the ten newest kept. A game's details list the copies and put one back.
- Covers: every game shows its official cover from IGDB, whole, on a label shaped like a game case, and its artwork behind its details. All 138 games have one.
- Cartouche shows only its own screens: LiveContainer's Apps and Settings tabs, the Open SideStore button and the app's page in the Settings app are gone. System opens over the console from the dock or the controller's Menu button, and OK or Menu closes it.
- The dock stays at the bottom, the shelves scrolling under it. From iOS 18, a game's details grow out of its cartridge.
- Game settings show one of nxnative's sections at a time, picked on a row of buttons or with L and R, the cloud saves last.
- The Mac filter also lists the games nxnative plays on the iPhone, which it plays on the Mac too.
