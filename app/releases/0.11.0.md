Your saves follow you, even after Cartouche is installed again, and Your first game no longer insists on a Mac.

- Saves: each game's saves stay in step with your saves folder, both ways. Before a game starts, Cartouche takes the newest copy when the folder has one, as after another iPhone played it; after the game, the saves that changed go. When both changed, the saves written last win, and the others stay in the folder as a copy with their date.
- Saves: a game installed again, as after Cartouche itself was installed again, gets its saves back. Your servers and folders, without their passwords, are now also kept in the keychain, which iOS keeps when an app is deleted, so a new install of Cartouche finds your storage again: open this version once before you uninstall Cartouche.
- Games: a game's sheet shows what came and what was kept, the last sync, and Sync with … now.
- Memory: with Game Mode, Cartouche has about 8 GiB on a 12 GB iPhone, and a game behind the console about 6 GiB. A game that needs more than it has behind the console starts in Cartouche's place; the others wait behind the console. A game's sheet compares its peak with the most memory Play can give it.
- Setup: in Your first game, No thanks skips the Mac's step, for games built already or without a Mac. The home's card has a × that takes it away, and System › Your first game opens the guide again. Send the steps to my Mac sends Cartouche Builder's page.
- iPhone Duo: unfolded, before your first game, the console takes the whole display, and a game's sheet moves beside it once you have one. All games' filters scroll from edge to edge.
- Mac: Cartouche Builder 0.3.0 opens a setup assistant at its first start, and 0.2.0 updates itself to it.
