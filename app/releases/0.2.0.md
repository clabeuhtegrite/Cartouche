A console's home, for every iPhone and the iPhone Duo.

- Home: your games on the first shelf and the games nxnative plays on the iPhone on the second, as cartridges. The game in focus shows under them with Play, or Import my copy, and its details. A dock leads to All games, Import, System, Apps and Settings.
- All games: every game of nxnative's list, 138 today, filtered by where nxnative plays it (iPhone, Mac, bring-up) or Switch 2, and searched by name or title ID.
- A game controller drives every screen: the D-pad moves, A plays, X opens a game's details, B goes back, L and R change the filter.
- iPhone Duo: Cartouche now uses the whole display, built with the iOS 27.2 SDK; larger cartridges unfolded, the shelves beside the game in focus when held sideways, details as a card.
- A game's details say what nxnative says of it: where it plays it, the engine, the game version and SDK of its profile, its title ID and other regions.
