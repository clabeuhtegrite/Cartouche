# 96. The Mac's builds in Cartouche: a game's own on its sheet, all of them in System

2026-11-13

With a Mac paired, a game's sheet in Cartouche shows the game's build in Cartouche Builder on that Mac, where it is and why it failed, a button that asks the Mac to build the game, at once or after the games of the batch running, as the game's own button there does, which decides whether it is built again, and one that stops its build, the Mac going on with the next game, or takes it out of the queue. **All the Mac's builds**, which the sheet and System open, under Storage, drive the rest of what the Mac's window does to its builds: the Mac and why it takes no build, **Build what is due** and **Stop all**, the game building and **Stop its build**, the games waiting their turn and **Remove**, the builds that failed and why, every game of the Mac with where it is, its button and **Follow** or **Stop following**, and the nightly build, on or off, and its time. The Mac's folders, its tools and an IPA's import stay on the Mac. Cartouche asks the Mac over the bridge, operations of its own (`builds`, `build`; `buildDue`, `stop`, `stopAll`, `follow`, `nightly`), every two seconds while the Mac builds and every ten otherwise, only while the sheet or the page shows; a Mac whose `info` does not say `hasBuilds` is never asked, and a Cartouche Builder that old is told to update; one that does not say `hasBuildsControl` is asked only its builds and a game's build, and is told to update for the rest. The Mac answers only while Cartouche Builder runs, with what its window shows; a game followed or not, once its library says so. ([`CartoucheBridgeBuilds.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheBridgeBuilds.swift), [`CartoucheMacBuildsView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheMacBuildsView.swift), [`CartoucheMacBuildsPage.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheMacBuildsPage.swift), [`BridgeBuilds.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/BridgeBuilds.swift))

**Why.** Ad asked to see and start the Mac's builds straight from the games' sheets when a Mac is connected (2026-11-13), then for a minimal management from a sheet and a complete one in System, in the same pull request (2026-11-14). The bridge already carries the Games and Saves folders, paired and encrypted, so the builds go the same way, to the player's own iPhones alone. An older Mac closes a session on an operation it does not know, hence `hasBuilds` and `hasBuildsControl` first. Asking only while a sheet or the page shows keeps the Mac's builds out of the console's home, where nothing else asks the Mac, and the Mac awake no longer than a player looks at it. The nightly time goes to the Mac once the wheel stops, so that the Mac sets its schedule once.
