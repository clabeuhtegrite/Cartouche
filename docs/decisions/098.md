# 98. Cartouche has LocalDevVPN connect by itself

2026-11-13

Cartouche has LocalDevVPN connect by itself when what reaches the iPhone through it starts with LocalDevVPN off: the sign-in and the registration of this iPhone, **Renew now** and an update, as the player asks for them; and, by itself, the renewal in the last days of the signature ([decision 88](../decisions.md)) and the sign-in again for a certificate iOS no longer accepts. It opens LocalDevVPN's own URL, `localdevvpn://enable?scheme=<Cartouche's URL scheme>`, which connects it and opens Cartouche again a second later, and waits for the tunnel 20 seconds at most, counting only its time in front, then goes on, connected or not, as before ([`CartoucheVPN.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheVPN.swift), [`CartoucheLocalDevVPN.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheLocalDevVPN.swift)). By itself, it does so only from the front, never over a game behind the console, and once in 6 hours at most. The automation of **Renew every night** runs LocalDevVPN's *Enable LocalDevVPN* action before Cartouche's *Refresh All Apps*, which waits for the tunnel 15 seconds at most ([LiveContainer patch 0015](../../app/patches/livecontainer/0015-wait-for-localdevvpns-tunnel-before-refresh-all-apps.patch)): the player no longer leaves LocalDevVPN connected all day.

This amends decision 88: the renewal Cartouche starts by itself no longer waits for the player to connect LocalDevVPN.

**Why.** Ad asked, on November 13, whether Cartouche could do without LocalDevVPN or carry it inside, without a VPN to set up. It cannot with a free account: iOS lets no app reach its own iPhone's services (lockdownd) without a tunnel, and a tunnel needs the Network Extension entitlement, which Apple gives no Personal Team, and which SideStore cannot sign in, since it signs only what the profile allows; a tunnel of any app also needs the player to allow its VPN configuration once. LocalDevVPN stays needed for the sign-in, renewals and updates, never to play or install a game. What could go is the player's part: up to 0.12.1, they connected LocalDevVPN in its app before each of these, came back, and left it on for the automation; the measurements of the week of November 10 showed it off on most copies when Cartouche came to the front in its last days. Ad chose both halves on November 13 ("Oui, les deux").
