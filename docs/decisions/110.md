# 110. The Home button over a game fades away, and a touch on its place brings it back

2026-11-14

The Home button at the bottom of the screen of a game behind the console ([decision 38](../decisions.md)) shows while the game starts, then for three seconds once the game shows, as the game comes back from the console, and as Cartouche comes back to the screen; then it fades away, to 2% of itself ([`CartoucheGameHost.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheGameHost.swift)). It stays where it was, at the bottom in the middle: a touch on its place brings it back for three seconds, and shows no console; a touch on it then shows the console, as before. The first time it fades away, a line over its place says how to bring it back, once. It stays while the game hides from a copy of the iPhone's screen ([decision 85](../decisions.md)), where there is no game to see, and while VoiceOver or Switch Control runs, which reach only the buttons on screen. A controller's Home button works as before.

**Why.** A player asked for the Home button to stay out of sight until they want it (Reddit, 2026-11-14): over a game played with a controller, where nxnative hides its controls on screen, it is all that is left over the picture. Ad chose a touch on its place rather than any touch on the screen (2026-11-14): with the controls on screen the player touches the screen all the time, and the button would come back with every touch, while on its place, where nxnative's controls leave room, the screen stays clean even then, at the cost of knowing where to touch, which the button shows as each game starts and the line says once. Cartouche never sees the touches on the game's scene, which iOS hands the game's process: faded to 2% rather than hidden or to nothing, the button still takes the touches on its place, since UIKit gives none to a view at 0.01 or less, and a button that took none could never come back.
