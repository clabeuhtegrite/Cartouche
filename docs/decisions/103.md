# 103. On an iPhone, a game that lays itself out upright turns upright too

2026-11-13

On an iPhone, a game turns upright too, as the player holds it, when it lays itself out upright: when its Info.plist allows it upright on an iPad, as nxnative's games do since its commit e2fadf2 of 4 November 2026, which puts their picture above their controls wherever their view is taller than wide. Behind the console, Cartouche turns the game's screen so ([`CartoucheGameOrientation.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheGameOrientation.swift)). In Cartouche's place, where iOS turns the game as its own Info.plist allows on an iPhone, sideways only for nxnative's, Cartouche's code in the game's process tells UIKit that the game allows upright too ([`CartoucheUprightGame.m`](../../launcher/Guest/CartoucheUprightGame.m)). A game built before turns sideways only, as its Info.plist says for an iPad too. While the game's picture is on a TV, the iPhone turns sideways only. On an iPad, a game turns as it allows there, and a game the player locked one way in LiveContainer's settings stays so.

**Why.** The player tried 0.12.0 on an iPhone Duo (2026-11-13): Donkey Kong Bananza, which started in Cartouche's place, the game's process behind the console having lost Increased Memory Limit, turned upright on the inner display, its picture above its controls, but stayed sideways on the outer one, as the game's Info.plist says for an iPhone; they chose to have the game follow the iPhone upright on the outer display and on other iPhones too, as on the inner one, behind the console and in Cartouche's place alike. nxnative keeps an iPhone sideways in a game's own app, but lays the game out upright wherever its view is taller than wide (its `DmLayout.h`), as the Duo's inner display shows, where iOS turns an app as it is held whatever the app allows. A game built before has no such layout: upright, its picture would stay in the middle and its controls, made to be held sideways, would run into each other on a narrow iPhone, as nxnative's controls alone do while the picture is on a TV.
