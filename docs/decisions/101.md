# 101. A game's log, shared from its sheet: as the game wrote it, or cleaned to Cartouche

2026-11-13

A game's sheet has **Share the log** once the game has written one ([`CartoucheGameLogView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheGameLogView.swift), [`CartoucheGameLogShare.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheGameLogShare.swift)). Its page offers two ways, and nothing leaves without the player's tap. **Send the log…** shares, with the share sheet, a zip of `log.txt` and `log.prev.txt` as the game wrote them, beside `about.txt`, a summary: the game, its build, Cartouche, iOS and the device, each session's dates and size, its crash line and the lines nxnative marks as problems. **Send to Cartouche** sends the server's problems route (`POST /v1/problems`, unchanged) the summary and the start and the end of each log, within its 60,000 characters, cleaned first: the folders of apps and every other path, addresses, the player's names, UUIDs, anything shaped like a key, the nickname and the memory a crash or a thread writes out are taken out, while the crash line, the registers, the backtraces and the module offsets stay; the player sees it first, and the server files it as an issue of the private repository, as a report. Cartouche's console on the Mac gets this page with its own reports, at step 8 of the plan of decision 95, with the log nxnative's app for the Mac writes; until then the sheet's row is the iPhone's alone.

**Why.** The owner accepted a contributor's issue #169 on 13 November, asking for both ways: testers and contributors debug a game without the player's iPhone. The owner then chose the log as the game wrote it for the share sheet, which goes only to whoever the player picks, and the log cleaned for Cartouche's server, which keeps to the rule of the reports (no secret, identifier or address) and of the measurements (no raw line of a log, [decision 35](../decisions.md)).
