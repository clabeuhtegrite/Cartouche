# 99. Reset Cartouche erases what the player put in, and starts Cartouche again as new

2026-11-13

System › **Reset Cartouche** erases everything the player put into Cartouche and everything it learned since, then starts it again on its console as a new player finds it the first time it opens after the Cartouche Installer, without a computer and without installing it again ([`CartoucheReset.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheReset.swift), [`CartoucheResetPlan.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheResetPlan.swift)). Erased: the games with their data folders, saves, settings, play time and keychain items, those of the games uninstalled with their saves kept too; the storage servers with their passwords and tokens; the Apple ID the built-in SideStore signed in with, its password and tokens, with SideStore's preferences and files; Cartouche's preferences, the names and covers given to the games, the guide's progress, the caches, the journal and the reports. Kept: the signing certificate and the pairing file, which only a computer gives back, with the protocol the built-in SideStore reads the pairing file by, without which it would ask for another one before iOS 26.4; what Apple's anisette server knows the iPhone by, and Apple's limit on sign-ins while it counts; the telemetry's number, its switch and the reports not sent yet; the player's storage folders and what they hold, even in Cartouche's own space in Files; in the app group, LiveContainer's preferences and SideStore's database; what iOS keeps of the app. The page names the games whose saves are not in the saves folder as they are here, and sends them there first if the player asks; the button asks twice, and waits for a game, an install, a sign-in, an update or a renewal under way; what could not be erased stops the reset with a report before Cartouche starts again.

**Why.** Ad asked for a button that resets the whole app to zero (2026-11-13), to try each version again from a fresh start without uninstalling Cartouche and installing it again: an uninstall erases the games but leaves the keychain, where Cartouche keeps its storage and SideStore the Apple ID, and installing again needs the computer, for the certificate and the pairing file. Ad chose to erase the Apple ID and keep the certificate: signing in again without the certificate the app is signed with would end, whenever the account's certificate is not one the app holds, in installing Cartouche again from a computer ([patch 0004](../../app/patches/sidestore/0004-never-revoke-a-certificate.patch)). Ad asked for the telemetry's number to stay, so that the dashboard follows the same copy of Cartouche across a reset; **Erase what this iPhone sent** still gives a new one.
