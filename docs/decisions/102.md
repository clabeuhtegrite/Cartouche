# 102. A game's sheet shows its testers' results in Cartouche's language

2026-11-13

Of a result Cartouche's game measurements made, Cartouche says the note and the engine itself, from the figures of its `measured`, in the words of `Cartouche.xcstrings` with the iPhone's numbers and dates ([`CartoucheMeasuredNote.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheMeasuredNote.swift)): how many iPhones, or iPads for an iPad's model, and sessions, when, the frame rate and the stutters, how many sessions ended badly and how, and the nxnative builds they reported, which the weekly sheets now carry in `measured` too (`endedBadly`, `nxnative`, `otherBuilds`, [`sheets.py`](../../telemetry/sheets.py)). What testers wrote themselves, an engine or a note, the iPhone translates as it does the descriptions ([decision 84](../decisions.md)), from iOS 18, once: its translations are kept with theirs; **Show the original** brings the English back. Each result's day shows in the player's language too.

**Why.** Ad saw the results of Mario Kart World's sheet in English in Cartouche in French (2026-11-13): the sentences the weekly workflow writes for the catalogue, in English, and the testers' own words. Cartouche says the measured ones exactly from their figures, as no translation would; what testers write cannot be known in advance, and the iPhone's translation needs no key nor server.
